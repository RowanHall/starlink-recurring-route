Mobile Starlink Performance over Recurring Route

CSC 466 · Department of Computer Science · University of Victoria

We are studying how Starlink (LEO satellite internet) performs when the receiver is on a moving vehicle. Our project uses about 1.5 years of network measurements collected from a school bus that drives the same route every weekday, to understand where and why performance changes along the way.
